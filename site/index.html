How I think about AI systems

Structural controls beat clever ones

A capability that does not exist cannot be misused. Detection is the layer everyone demonstrates and the layer that fails first.

Authority is a record, not a mood

Approving and doing are separate, attributable events. An AI proposal is never an approval, however confident it sounds.

Fail closed, and say why

When a system cannot prove something, it stops and records the reason. A refusal is an audited outcome, not an error to retry around.

Verify the result, not the report

A 202 is not a label and an agent's claim is not a test result. Read it back, re-run it, then believe it.

Publish the number that did not flatter

Honest maturity labels (demonstrated, built, specified) do more for credibility than any feature list.

Check that the owner can still act

A control that blocks everything passes every "is it blocked?" test. Always verify the legitimate path works too.
